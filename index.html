FAQ

Do you travel outside Guangzhou?

Yes. I can accompany you to nearby cities such as Foshan, Dongguan and Shenzhen. Travel costs and extra time are quoted separately, so please message me first.

Can you interpret at the Canton Fair?

Yes. I can accompany you through the halls, interpret with exhibitors, and take notes and photos of the products and quotes you’re interested in. Book early, as availability is limited during the fair.

Which languages do you cover?

Mandarin Chinese and English as my main pair, plus Cantonese. I also speak basic Spanish, but I’d recommend Spanish only for simple conversations.

Can you also help negotiate prices?

I can support the negotiation by communicating your position accurately and sharing market insight from my sourcing experience. The final decision is always yours.
